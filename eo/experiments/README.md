# `eo/experiments/`: one-off analyses, by topic

Each script produced one result or one set of figures and is kept as the record of how. They are
not a pipeline: nothing under `eo/` depends on them, except that `scripts/tokenize_clean.py` borrows
`clamp_test`. Reusable logic belongs in a package under `eo/`, and CLI entry points in `eo/scripts/`.

| Folder | What it holds | Env |
|---|---|---|
| `tokenizer_contract/` | The bring-up that established the preprocessing contract (`s3*.py`), the DiVAE clamp investigation (`clamp_test`, `clamp_generated`, `code_value_test`), S1 metrics, and small data checks. `clamp_test` is also a shared helper: it decodes tokens with and without the clamp | `mor` |
| `s2_offset/` | The +1000 DN BOA offset in S2L2A: the scans, its effect on tokens and routing, the checks behind `val_clean`, and the upstream-version check `s2_offset_train_check.py` | `mor`, some `.venv` |
| `generation/` | Generation against held-out truth: CE vs decoded metrics (`p1_ce_vs_decoded`), the any-to-any grid and its controls and chains (`s12_*`), and the head-to-head with TerraMind (`tm_compare_*`) | `.venv` to generate, `mor` to decode |
| `bigearthnet/` | BigEarthNet: tokens and round trips, spatial leakage, test analyses, forgetting, summaries and figures. `ben_t360_summary.py` is the current report | `.venv` and `mor` |
| `pastis/` | PASTIS-R inspection | `mor` |
| `segmentation/` | Paper A's A2, label maps rendered as RGB and tokenized (Step 0, 2026-11-15). Perfect-token round-trip ceilings through Cosmos's discrete tokenizers on PASTIS and TerraMesh LULC, by palette and grid, plus the Phase-6 class-per-cell ceilings (`rgb_map_ceiling`). Two decoding rules (nearest colour, and a region rule that keeps edge blends off third classes) and code statistics against the training folds and LULC (`rgb_map_codes`). A small palette-agnostic map autoencoder with an FSQ bottleneck (`rgb_map_ae`). Step 1 (2026-11-18/19), the map-tokenizer shortlist, every number through `eo/maps/`'s harness. Agent A, off-the-shelf Cosmos: units on the TITAN V under the watchdog (`step1_A_queue`); the rule chosen on train_sub, the read, determinism, the transposed control, native-256 crops, val's own rules as a diagnostic, and the codebook prune with its loader `load_pruned` (`step1_A_run`); its tables (`step1_A_table`). Agent B, the dedicated f16 tokenizer: the model and its loader (`step1_B_tok`); training on palette-shuffled renders of the `fit` maps (`step1_B_train`); a read with its controls (`step1_B_read`); a training-step benchmark (`step1_B_bench`); curve tables and figures (`step1_B_curves`); an A100 smoke (`step1_B_rcp_smoke`); one RCP job, train then read (`step1_B_rcp_run`); runs back to back in one RCP job (`step1_B_rcp_chain`); TerraMind's own LULC tokenizer as a reference (`step1_B_ref_terramind`; `a2seg` has terratorch); its candidate table (`step1_B_table`). Agent C, the dedicated f8 tokenizer: the model and its loader (`step1_C_tok`); training (`step1_C_train`); reads with controls (`step1_C_read`); an A100 smoke (`step1_C_smoke`); the same reads on an A100 (`step1_C_rcpread`); its tables and freezing records (`step1_C_table`). The orchestrator: the harness's A100 smoke (`step1_O_rcp_smoke`); an independent re-score of an agent's candidate with the token cache off (`step1_O_rescore`); rule 1.3 over every read, the table and the freezing records (`step1_O_shortlist --freeze`). Step 2 (2026-11-19), GEO-Bench-2's data and the input contracts (readers in `eo/geobench/`, CLIs `eo/scripts/tokenize_geobench.py`, `tokenize_maps.py`, and `tokenize_train_lulc_maps.py` for 2.6, prepared, not run): the splits against Table 2 and PASTIS-R, and the Hub head's PASTIS read remotely (`step2_splits`); the B01/B09 imputation measured on BEN and TerraMesh against the true bands, its linear fit, and BEN token roots for the functional test (`step2_impute`); the S2 contract's round trips with the broken controls, token-usage JSD against TerraMesh, and CloudSen12's rule 2.4 (`step2_contract`); the token roots' record, sizes and hashes (`step2_roots`). A2's base (2026-11-19): block alignment by position on B10cL-tr-x10-NC against T360 (`a2_base_alignment`, `.venv`). Step 3 (2026-11-19), the engineering between the tokens and the first fine-tune (map vocabulary `eo/data/map_vocab.py`, sequences `eo/data/seg_dataset.py`, generation and scoring `eo/eval/seg.py` with its CLI `eo/scripts/eval_seg.py`, the `.venv` half of the gate `eo/scripts/verify_seg_model.py`): the class-prior control on the map-extended base's untrained rows, read against a uniform-random and the best constant map on the same ground truth (`step3_prior_read`, a2seg) | `.venv` (Step 0, Cosmos JITs in bf16); `a2seg` (Steps 1–2; `mor-eo` on RCP) |
| `pretrained_init/` | Pretrained-body inits (CLEVR, SmolLM): layer drop, comparisons, slides; how much faster pretraining is from a borrowed body on the val runs' stored curves, stage 1 counted at its FLOPs (`a03_speedup`, Paper A's A0.3); the Coords-free init comparison on train data, random init against SmolLM-135M's body on scenes and FLOPs (`nc_init_compare`), and its first 3,300 steps with and without stage 1, read by the rule fixed before the no-stage-1 run (`nc_stage1_compare`, 2026-11-18) | `.venv` |
| `base/` | Paper A's A0, the base (wave 1 of 2026-11-11): A0.2's screen of a train-split model against T360 and B10c, paired per token, with the grid (`a0_read screen`), and A0.1's Coords pairs, per row on the image tokens (`a0_read coords`) | `.venv` |
| `routing/` | Depth sweeps with random and learned routers: reports and numbers. Paper B's B0 audits: the router's state in every cell (`b0_states`), the forces on it (`b0_forces`), and replays read at 10-step and one-update resolution, with their rules (`b0_replay`); cross-seed agreement and stability on time means (`b0_agree`); per-modality and token-id tables against the router (`b0_tables`: `build`, `token`, `read`, `fit`); the random router's depth landscape with the context left alone, and the additive estimate against real tables (`b0_landscape`); what a router deciding after each pass could know: hidden states after layer 0 and after pass 1 against each token's gain from extra passes (`b0_perpass`: `capture`, `selftest`, `fit`); criterion 4's router-free measure, its reliability, and the regret of a run's allocation at its own load against the gain-optimal one (an exact 5 × 3 transportation solver) with its slot-relabelling null, that test's power and the test at single checkpoints (`b0_criterion4`: `selftest`, `read`, `extra`, `power`, `early`, `freeze`); every number of the audits' results from the stored readings, the few no reading holds recomputed and checked against the readings that do, and their figures (`b0_report`: `numbers`, `figs`). B3's train-split control (wave 1): a run's per-update depth shares read for ties and their ringing on time means, with planted controls and the random-router twin as the real one (`b3_depth_shares`), and the learned router's held-out edge over the random one through training, from both runs' logged evals, q1's val numbers reproduced first, and as E1 paired per token at 1980 and 3300 with a row bootstrap (`b3_edge`, `e1`); a cell's own seed gap for B3's rule, from two random-router seeds' routed passes, which reproduces the default 0.006 from A10c and A10c-s2 (`b3_edge`, `gap`), and B3's rule applied with that gap to each run's stored landscape reading, per checkpoint and with the 3300 gap at both (`b3_edge`, `rule`); whether two seeds of a learned router learn one depth table, from their per-update depth shares, with B0 Step 4's agreement test and a random-router run as the control that must not agree (`b3_seed_tables`); `e1` and `gap` pair runs scored with a modality dropped (`eval_checkpoint.py --drop-modalities`) only with runs that dropped the same, and skip the dropped modality. B3's close-out (2026-11-15): criteria 2 and 4 on a cell's learned runs, criterion 2 from 30-update block means of the per-update depth shares with Welch intervals, criterion 4 by B0's regret test in both frames, and criterion 4's measure frozen between a cell's two random seeds, with planted controls (`b3_criteria`); B3's gate-force prediction on data seen once, which reconstructs which train rows a run saw from its sampler order (checked against its logged per-update S1GRD counts), builds a mini root of seen, never-seen and held-out rows and runs B0's force unit on each (`b3_forces_train`: `select`, `build`, `unit`, `read`); a val run's allocation read from its router captures on time windows, which reproduces agree.json's A10c means first (`b3_a10cnc_allocation`); a vanilla paired per token with a cell's routed passes and its random backbones forced to each depth, tested with a random seed in the vanilla's place, and E8's pair, an 11-layer vanilla scored with Coords against it per row on the image tokens with A0.1's identity check (`b3_vanilla5`). Paper B's B1 (2026-11-12 to 16): intervention 1's compute target, its smoke replays (a learned run's checkpoint-1650 resumed for 200 updates under the target or, as the control, its balancing loss) and arm B's smoke from step 0 (`--scratch`), read by rules fixed before they ran, the confidence term's on the hard mean (`b1_ct_smoke`), and a toy of its price loop that chose the controller's gains, with the hard measure's quantised plant (`b1_price_loop`); the convergence criteria as pass or fail for an arm of runs, with C4 a signal since 2026-11-16, each arm read over its own windows from its switch on, arm A's stored readings reproduced first (`paper_b_criteria`); arm C's E1 paired per token with the cell's random routers, arm A, the matched vanilla, the random backbones forced to depth 2 and the other C units, with the pairing's control (`b1_c_read`) | `.venv` |
| `temporal/` | The temporal stage's checks: S1 alignment on train (`s1_dates`, `mor`; `s1_functional`) and what T360 does with dates it never saw together (`t360_context`, `t360_lattice`, `t360_best_layouts`), why DEM differs between a location's dates on train (`dem_dates`), whether a row's date is its S2 image's (`s2_dates`, `mor`), and Step 8's RCP bring-up: T360-T's memory, speed and data wait on one A100 (`a100_bench`) and the two-rank mid-epoch resume, read from the dataset's micro-batch trace (`resume_trace`); Step 9's pairing of a checkpoint's readings with the extended base's, per location (`pilot_compare`), its forgetting paired per token with the base's against the margin (`forgetting_compare`), and a checkpoint's readings set against the margin table (`pilot_margins`) | `.venv`, `s1_dates` and `s2_dates` in `mor`; `a100_bench` in `mor-eo` on RCP |
| `continuous_io/` | The continuous input/output pilot (`cio_*`): data, training, decoding, analysis, slides | `.venv`, `mor` to decode |
| `figures/` | `midterm_figures.py` (the house plotting style, imported by other slide scripts) and `t360_slides.py` | `.venv` |

Run a script from the repo root, for example:

    .venv/bin/python eo/experiments/routing/depth_sweep_report.py

The `s3*.py` scripts and a few others import `terramesh_tok` directly, so they need `PYTHONPATH=eo`.
Most write under `/data/enric`: large outputs never go under `/home`.
