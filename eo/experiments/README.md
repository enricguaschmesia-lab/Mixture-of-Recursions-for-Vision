# `eo/experiments/`: one-off analyses, by topic

Each script produced one result or one set of figures and is kept as the record of how. They are
not a pipeline: nothing under `eo/` depends on them, except that `scripts/tokenize_clean.py` borrows
`clamp_test`. Reusable logic belongs in a package under `eo/`, and CLI entry points in `eo/scripts/`.

| Folder | What it holds | Env |
|---|---|---|
| `tokenizer_contract/` | The bring-up that established the preprocessing contract (`s3*.py`), the DiVAE clamp investigation (`clamp_test`, `clamp_generated`, `code_value_test`), S1 metrics, and small data checks. `clamp_test` is also a shared helper: it decodes tokens with and without the clamp | `mor` |
| `s2_offset/` | The +1000 DN BOA offset in S2L2A: the scans, its effect on tokens and routing, the checks behind `val_clean`, and the upstream-version check `s2_offset_train_check.py` | `mor`, some `.venv` |
| `generation/` | Generation against held-out truth: CE vs decoded metrics (`p1_ce_vs_decoded`), the any-to-any grid and its controls and chains (`s12_*`), and the head-to-head with TerraMind (`tm_compare_*`) | `.venv` to generate, `mor` to decode |
| `bigearthnet/` | BigEarthNet: tokens and round trips, spatial leakage, test analyses, forgetting, summaries and figures. `ben_t360_summary.py` is the current report | `.venv` and `mor` |
| `pastis/` | PASTIS-R inspection | `mor` |
| `segmentation/` | Paper A's A2, label maps rendered as RGB and tokenized (Step 0, 2026-11-15). Perfect-token round-trip ceilings through Cosmos's discrete tokenizers on PASTIS and TerraMesh LULC, by palette and grid, plus the Phase-6 class-per-cell ceilings (`rgb_map_ceiling`). Two decoding rules (nearest colour, and a region rule that keeps edge blends off third classes) and code statistics against the training folds and LULC (`rgb_map_codes`). A small palette-agnostic map autoencoder with an FSQ bottleneck (`rgb_map_ae`) | `.venv` (Cosmos JITs in bf16) |
| `pretrained_init/` | Pretrained-body inits (CLEVR, SmolLM): layer drop, comparisons, slides; how much faster pretraining is from a borrowed body on the val runs' stored curves, stage 1 counted at its FLOPs (`a03_speedup`, Paper A's A0.3) | `.venv` |
| `base/` | Paper A's A0, the base (wave 1 of 2026-11-11): A0.2's screen of a train-split model against T360 and B10c, paired per token, with the grid (`a0_read screen`), and A0.1's Coords pairs, per row on the image tokens (`a0_read coords`) | `.venv` |
| `routing/` | Depth sweeps with random and learned routers: reports and numbers. Paper B's B0 audits: the router's state in every cell (`b0_states`), the forces on it (`b0_forces`), and replays read at 10-step and one-update resolution, with their rules (`b0_replay`); cross-seed agreement and stability on time means (`b0_agree`); per-modality and token-id tables against the router (`b0_tables`: `build`, `token`, `read`, `fit`); the random router's depth landscape with the context left alone, and the additive estimate against real tables (`b0_landscape`); what a router deciding after each pass could know: hidden states after layer 0 and after pass 1 against each token's gain from extra passes (`b0_perpass`: `capture`, `selftest`, `fit`); criterion 4's router-free measure, its reliability, and the regret of a run's allocation at its own load against the gain-optimal one (an exact 5 × 3 transportation solver) with its slot-relabelling null, that test's power and the test at single checkpoints (`b0_criterion4`: `selftest`, `read`, `extra`, `power`, `early`, `freeze`); every number of the audits' results from the stored readings, the few no reading holds recomputed and checked against the readings that do, and their figures (`b0_report`: `numbers`, `figs`). B3's train-split control (wave 1): a run's per-update depth shares read for ties and their ringing on time means, with planted controls and the random-router twin as the real one (`b3_depth_shares`), and the learned router's held-out edge over the random one through training, from both runs' logged evals, q1's val numbers reproduced first, and as E1 paired per token at 1980 and 3300 with a row bootstrap (`b3_edge`, `e1`); a cell's own seed gap for B3's rule, from two random-router seeds' routed passes, which reproduces the default 0.006 from A10c and A10c-s2 (`b3_edge`, `gap`), and B3's rule applied with that gap to each run's stored landscape reading, per checkpoint and with the 3300 gap at both (`b3_edge`, `rule`); whether two seeds of a learned router learn one depth table, from their per-update depth shares, with B0 Step 4's agreement test and a random-router run as the control that must not agree (`b3_seed_tables`); `e1` and `gap` pair runs scored with a modality dropped (`eval_checkpoint.py --drop-modalities`) only with runs that dropped the same, and skip the dropped modality | `.venv` |
| `temporal/` | The temporal stage's checks: S1 alignment on train (`s1_dates`, `mor`; `s1_functional`) and what T360 does with dates it never saw together (`t360_context`, `t360_lattice`, `t360_best_layouts`), why DEM differs between a location's dates on train (`dem_dates`), whether a row's date is its S2 image's (`s2_dates`, `mor`), and Step 8's RCP bring-up: T360-T's memory, speed and data wait on one A100 (`a100_bench`) and the two-rank mid-epoch resume, read from the dataset's micro-batch trace (`resume_trace`); Step 9's pairing of a checkpoint's readings with the extended base's, per location (`pilot_compare`), its forgetting paired per token with the base's against the margin (`forgetting_compare`), and a checkpoint's readings set against the margin table (`pilot_margins`) | `.venv`, `s1_dates` and `s2_dates` in `mor`; `a100_bench` in `mor-eo` on RCP |
| `continuous_io/` | The continuous input/output pilot (`cio_*`): data, training, decoding, analysis, slides | `.venv`, `mor` to decode |
| `figures/` | `midterm_figures.py` (the house plotting style, imported by other slide scripts) and `t360_slides.py` | `.venv` |

Run a script from the repo root, for example:

    .venv/bin/python eo/experiments/routing/depth_sweep_report.py

The `s3*.py` scripts and a few others import `terramesh_tok` directly, so they need `PYTHONPATH=eo`.
Most write under `/data/enric`: large outputs never go under `/home`.
